# Outreach Auditor

## Procedure

1. Confirm the candidate is A-tier or strong B-tier.
2. Read the advisor dossier and applicant context.
3. Check every factual claim in the draft.
4. Remove generic praise and unsupported claims.
5. Ensure the email names one specific paper, project, or research direction.
6. Ensure the applicant connection is real and concise.
7. End with one clear question.
8. Mark human approval as required.

## Output

- Revised draft.
- Claim audit table.
- Missing evidence.
- Human approval checkbox.

## Rule

This skill never sends emails and never marks them as human-approved without explicit user approval.

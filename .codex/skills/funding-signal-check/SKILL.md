# Funding Signal Check

## Procedure

1. Read the advisor dossier or candidate row.
2. Check official lab/faculty pages for recruiting statements.
3. Search NSF Award Search for CS, statistics, methodology, and interdisciplinary awards.
4. Search NIH RePORTER for biostatistics, biomedical ML, public health, genomics, epidemiology, and clinical collaboration signals.
5. Check center, training grant, or lab funding pages when relevant.
6. Interpret funding as a signal, not a guarantee of admission or support.
7. Use `templates/funding-check.md` and update `data/advisors.csv`.

## Output

- Strong / medium / weak / unknown funding signal.
- Source URLs.
- What can be inferred.
- What remains unknown.

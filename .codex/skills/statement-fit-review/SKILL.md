# Statement Fit Review

## Procedure

1. Read the applicant context and relevant advisor dossiers.
2. Identify claims in the statement that are broad, unsupported, or disconnected from target advisors.
3. Map each research claim to applicant evidence and advisor/program evidence.
4. Suggest revisions that increase specificity without exaggeration.
5. Flag any sentence that sounds like the applicant read a paper they did not actually read.
6. Keep SOP work secondary to advisor fit unless the user asks otherwise.

## Output

- Claim audit table.
- Strong advisor-specific connection points.
- Weak or generic sentences.
- Suggested revised paragraphs or bullets.
- Missing evidence to collect.

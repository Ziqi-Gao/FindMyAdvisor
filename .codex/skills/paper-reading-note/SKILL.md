# Paper Reading Note

Adapted from PaperPilot and Research-Pilot style reading workflows.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the paper belongs to the intended faculty member.
2. Record title, authors, year, venue, DOI or URL, and source checked date.
3. Summarize the problem, methods, data, and contribution.
4. Explain how the paper connects to the applicant's projects or interests.
5. Identify one specific outreach hook and one intelligent question.
6. Mark whether the paper was fully read, abstract-read, or metadata-only.

## Rule

Do not claim to have read full text if only metadata or abstract was available.

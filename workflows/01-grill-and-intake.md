# Grill And Intake Workflow

Adapted from the `grill-with-docs` pattern.

## Goal

Build a precise applicant profile before searching advisors. Advisor search quality depends on the applicant's actual evidence, not broad interests.

## Inputs

- CV
- Transcript notes
- Research summary
- Project descriptions
- Writing sample or papers
- Constraints and preferences

## Procedure

1. Read `docs/CONTEXT.md` and any applicant materials.
2. Ask targeted questions until research interests are concrete enough to search.
3. Convert each project into advisor-search keywords.
4. Identify weak spots that may affect program choice.
5. Update `docs/CONTEXT.md` with facts and open questions.
6. Do not start large advisor searches until the profile has enough signal.

## Output

- Updated `docs/CONTEXT.md`.
- 10-30 advisor-search keywords grouped by CS, Statistics, Biostatistics, and interdisciplinary areas.
- A list of missing materials or profile unknowns.

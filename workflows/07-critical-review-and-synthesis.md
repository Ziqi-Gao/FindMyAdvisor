# Critical Review And Synthesis Workflow

Adapted from co-researcher and academic research suite workflows.

## Goal

Use research synthesis to improve advisor fit decisions and later SOP strategy.

## Use When

- Multiple faculty work in adjacent areas.
- You need to compare subfields across CS, Statistics, and Biostatistics.
- A candidate looks exciting but fit is fuzzy.
- You need an SOP research narrative later.

## Procedure

1. Build a small bibliography for a research cluster.
2. Identify common methods, datasets, questions, and venues.
3. Map the applicant's projects to the cluster.
4. Identify gaps where the applicant needs more reading or a better framing.
5. Convert synthesis into advisor fit evidence and possible SOP language.

## Output

- Cluster summary.
- Faculty comparison table.
- Applicant-positioning notes.
- Unknowns and next papers to read.

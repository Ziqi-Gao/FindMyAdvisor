# Funding And Availability Workflow

## Goal

Estimate whether a faculty member has active research momentum and plausible capacity. Do not infer guaranteed PhD slots from grants alone.

## Checks

1. Faculty or lab page recruiting statement.
2. Program admissions page.
3. NSF Award Search.
4. NIH RePORTER for biostatistics, biomedical ML, epidemiology, genomics, and public health.
5. Center grants, training grants, industry partnerships, or lab announcements.
6. Recent students or postdocs.

## Interpretation

- Strong signal: active grants, recent papers, current students, and clear admissions route.
- Medium signal: recent work but unclear recruiting or funding.
- Weak signal: old grants, stale lab page, no recent students, or unclear program route.
- Unknown: evidence unavailable.

## Output

Use `templates/funding-check.md` and update `data/advisors.csv`.

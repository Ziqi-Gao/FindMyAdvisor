# Professor Fit Dossier Workflow

Adapted from professor-fit-analyser style workflows.

## Goal

Turn a promising candidate into a decision-ready dossier.

## Required Checks

- Research overlap with applicant projects.
- Recent publications and whether the current direction is still active.
- Lab and student signal.
- Funding signal.
- Program and admissions route.
- Advising and availability risks.
- Outreach angle.

## Fit Score

Use a 0-10 score, but explain the score with evidence.

- 9-10: strong topic overlap, recent evidence, plausible admissions route, strong outreach angle.
- 7-8: good fit with some unknowns.
- 5-6: plausible but weak or indirect fit.
- 0-4: remove unless new evidence appears.

## Dossier Output

Use `templates/advisor-dossier.md`.

# Program Requirements Audit

## Goal

Avoid applying based on advisor fit alone when the program path, deadline, requirements, or faculty contact policy makes the application risky.

## Check

- Program name and degree.
- Deadline and timezone.
- Application URL.
- Faculty contact policy.
- Direct-admit, rotation, committee, or program-level admission.
- GRE policy.
- TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo policy.
- Recommendation letter count.
- Statement requirements.
- Writing sample requirement.
- Fee waiver availability.
- Funding guarantee or norms.
- International applicant notes.

## Output

Update `data/programs.csv` and `data/application-crm.csv`. Mark unknowns with source URLs and last-checked dates.
